01 Overview

The month the funnel started working — and we could finally prove it.

June was a breakthrough on the thing we can control right now: measuring properly. The campaign had been sending paid traffic to a gallery page with no enquiry form. In June we rebuilt conversion tracking and, right at month-end, took the new venue-hire landing page live. The rebuilt tracking is the win: it now captures 23 genuine form enquiries for the period where Google's native form-submit tracking was seeing just four — so for the first time we're counting real leads accurately. The new landing page and redesigned form only just went live, so their lift on conversion is still ahead of us — that's July's story.

On efficiency, the ads worked harder per dollar. On roughly a third of May's budget, cost per lead came in around $166 — below May's $236 and April's $201 — and cost per click fell to $1.76 from $2.33, roughly a third more clicks for every dollar. Cost per lead is still higher than we ultimately want — and the biggest lever isn't clicks, it's completion: on the old page the enquiry form was buried and hard to reach, and around 96 people who started an enquiry in June didn't finish it. The new landing page and redesigned form target exactly that gap, and lifting completion is what takes cost per lead a lot lower on the same spend. On the organic side, SEO continues to build: organic search rebounded +24% from April to May to its strongest level in months and now drives 37% of all traffic as the most engaged audience on the site. Year on year, form leads are up 77% and lead value up 90%.

The web foundation laid over recent months — PHP 8.2, LiteSpeed caching, database clean-up, Yoast SEO and 22 rewritten meta descriptions — is what makes this compound. We head into July positioned to scale: restore the Google budget onto a page that now converts, switch Meta on, and keep the organic curve climbing.

Our focus in June was not to chase volume, but to fix what happens after the click. With the budget deliberately trimmed to about a third of May's, we still cut cost per click and cost per lead — the ads are working harder per dollar. With the landing page now live and Meta about to switch on, restoring budget from here should convert into materially stronger lead volume.

The headline isn't volume, it's efficiency. We cut spend to about 40% of May's budget, yet still generated 23 leads — 57% of May's 40. 05 SEO, website & organic search

Organic is quietly becoming the strongest channel — and the most engaged.

Organic is compounding because of work done over the past few months, not overnight. Starting from a site that had no SEO plugin at all, we configured Yoast end to end, rewrote 22 meta descriptions, verified Search Console and submitted the sitemap, and shipped the technical foundation — PHP 8.2, LiteSpeed caching and a database clean-up — that makes pages fast enough to rank. The payoff is showing: organic search rebounded +24% from April to May (1,686 → 2,092 sessions), now drives 37% of all site traffic, and organic visitors are the most engaged audience on the site (engagement near 59%, well above the 46–51% site-wide) — high-intent traffic at zero click cost. This is the channel that keeps paying back long after the work is done.

The biggest single opportunity in the account

119 people started an enquiry in June — only 23 finished. That's a gap of roughly 96 would-be leads walking away mid-form. The old form sat on a heavy WPBakery page that was slow and awkward on mobile. The new Elementor landing page and redesigned enquiry form — shorter, clearer, mobile-first — went live at month-end specifically to close this gap. If the new form lifts completion even modestly, lead volume climbs — and cost per lead drops with it — at zero extra ad spend. This is the big lever for July.

▸ Our custom "enquiry submitted" event captured all 23 enquiries where GA4's native form-submit tracking saw only 4 — the rebuilt tracking is far more complete. Both enquiry forms are counted in Google Ads: "Lead from pages other than contact us" (13) plus "Contact Us Page Lead" (8) = the 21 form conversions Google credits. A tracking clean-up is queued — several conversion actions currently flag as "misconfigured" in the account.
